Hisab — Privacy Policy

App ID: com.nextgenai.hisab · Last updated: 26 September 2026
Short version: Hisab is an offline-first personal finance app. Your financial data stays on your device. We do not run servers and we never collect or sell your personal information. Optional cloud backup and in-app ads are described below.

1. Who we are

Hisab (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal finance application for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. For any questions, contact us at tanim.cse.buet@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Hisab is offline-first. The transactions, accounts, loans, notes, savings and other records you enter are stored locally on your device in an on-device database. This data is not transmitted to us and we have no access to it.

3. Optional cloud backup

If you choose to back up your data, the app can upload an encrypted backup file to a cloud storage account that you connect and control (such as Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox). This is entirely optional and user-initiated. The backup lives in your own cloud account; we do not receive a copy and cannot read it. Your use of that cloud service is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy.

4. Advertising

The free version of Hisab shows ads served by Google AdMob. To provide ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information from your device, which may include device identifiers (such as an advertising ID), IP address, general (coarse) location, and app-interaction and diagnostic data. This is used to serve and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalize them.

  • We configure AdMob with a maximum ad content rating of PG (Parental Guidance) and block sensitive ad categories including gambling, alcohol, consumer loans, dating, and similar.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are shown a Google-certified consent prompt (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are served, and you can change your choice later from the app’s settings.
  • In supported US states you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data from the app.
  • You can remove ads permanently via the in-app “Remove ads” purchase.

For details on how Google uses data, see Google’s Privacy & Terms.

5. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (for example, removing ads) are processed by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store; we do not receive or store your payment details.

6. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not have a backend server that receives your financial data.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

7. Children

Hisab is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices & rights

  • Delete your data at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app; local data is removed with it.
  • Manage or withdraw ad consent from the app’s privacy settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID from your device’s system settings.
  • Remove any cloud backup directly from your connected cloud account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tanim.cse.buet@gmail.com.

  • ক্লাউড ব্যাকআপ: আপনি চাইলে আপনার নিজের Google Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox অ্যাকাউন্টে ব্যাকআপ রাখতে পারেন—এটি সম্পূর্ণ ঐচ্ছিক এবং আপনার নিয়ন্ত্রণে।
